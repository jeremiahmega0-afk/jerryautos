Help Center

How does Jerry Autos verify listings?
Every vehicle on Jerry Autos undergoes a comprehensive 150-point physical inspection and full history report before being approved on the marketplace.
How do I download my invoice?
Go to "My Orders" in the sidebar, find your order, and click the Invoice button to download a PDF receipt.
Can I negotiate the price with a seller?
Yes! Use the "Contact Seller" button on any listing to open a direct chat and negotiate the price or ask questions about the vehicle.
What payment methods are accepted?
Jerry Autos supports bank transfers, Paystack, Flutterwave, and USSD payments. All transactions are processed securely through our escrow system.
