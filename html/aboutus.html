About Jerry Autos

Redefining Vehicle Commerce in Africa

Jerry Autos is more than just a marketplace — we are a movement dedicated to transforming how vehicles are bought and sold across Africa. Founded with a bold vision, we combine cutting-edge technology with deep automotive expertise to create a platform that prioritizes trust, security, and convenience.

Our platform serves as a bridge between honest sellers and serious buyers. Every vehicle listing undergoes a rigorous verification process, ensuring that what you see is exactly what you get. We've built robust tools that make searching, comparing, and transacting seamless — whether you're a first-time buyer or a seasoned dealer.

At Jerry Autos, innovation drives everything we do. From AI-powered vehicle recommendations to secure in-app payment solutions, we continuously evolve to meet the needs of our growing community. Our commitment is simple: to make vehicle commerce safe, transparent, and accessible for everyone.

Meet the Founder

Ajayi Jeremiah

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Ajayi Jeremiah's journey began with a deep-rooted passion for automobiles and a fascination with technology's power to solve real-world problems. Growing up, he witnessed firsthand the challenges people faced when trying to buy or sell vehicles — opaque pricing, fraudulent listings, and a lack of trust between parties. These experiences ignited a fire within him to build something better.

With a background spanning software engineering, product design, and automotive industry insights, Jeremiah assembled a world-class team and poured his energy into creating Jerry Autos. His leadership philosophy centers on radical transparency, customer obsession, and continuous innovation. Under his guidance, the company has grown from a simple idea into one of Africa's most promising vehicle marketplace platforms.

"I believe that everyone deserves a safe, fair, and seamless experience when buying or selling a vehicle. Jerry Autos was born from that belief — and every feature we build reflects our unwavering commitment to our users."
